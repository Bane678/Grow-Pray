Questions

Everything else you're wondering

Is Grow Pray really free?
Yes. Prayer times, tracking, the garden, streaks, Qibla, tasbih and duas are free forever, with no ads. An optional premium subscription unlocks cosmetic extras like the Golden tree and helps support development.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once your location is set, prayer times are calculated on your device — no connection needed. Your garden, streaks and history are stored locally too.
Which calculation methods and madhabs are supported?
You can choose from the major calculation methods (including Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura and more) and set Hanafi or Standard (Shafi'i/Maliki/Hanbali) for Asr timing in Settings.
Is my data private?
Completely. Your prayer history never leaves your phone — there are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics tracking your worship. Your relationship with Allah stays between you and Him.
What happens if I miss a prayer?
Your tree begins to wither — a gentle nudge, not a punishment. Streak freezes can protect your progress, and Rest Mode pauses everything during travel, illness or menstruation without losing your streaks.
Is there an Android version?
Grow Pray is iOS-only for now. An Android version is on the roadmap — email us at support@growpray.com and we'll let you know when it arrives.
